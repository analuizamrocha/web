## Executive takeaway

Ana Luiza already has strong local and topical visibility. The June–July report shows 66 terms in the Top 3 and another 30 in positions 4–10, with 39 improvements against eight declines. The next stage is not to expand the tracker indiscriminately or publish generic posts. It is to protect the local acquisition terms, move treatment searches from the homepage to the correct treatment pages, and turn existing visibility into consultation journeys.

The clearest warning is `proctologia curitiba`, which fell from position 3 to 9. The clearest validation is that high-intent treatment queries improved: `fissura anal laser curitiba` reached position 1 and `cirurgia hemorroidas curitiba` reached position 2. Existing treatment clusters are working, but page ownership and conversion alignment still need attention.

## Strategic diagnosis

### 1. The site already owns more than brand demand

This is not the ICAB pattern where brand was healthy but non-brand was missing. Ana Luiza already ranks strongly for both the specialty and several procedures. Brand terms should remain health checks, but the growth dashboard should focus on local acquisition and treatment intent.

### 2. Page ownership is still the main SEO constraint

The May report found 59 of 84 ranked terms pointing to the homepage, including treatment queries. The June–July gains suggest authority is increasing, but the correct success metric is not merely a higher position: it is the correct treatment page ranking for its own cluster.

For the next report, add a “Found URL” review to every priority-1 keyword. A treatment term ranking through the homepage is a partial win and an internal-linking/on-page opportunity.

### 3. The blog is substantial; consolidation now matters more than raw volume

The repository contains 44 posts, including multiple pages for coceira anal, fissura anal, hemorroidas, HPV, cisto pilonidal, and fístulas. New posts should only be created after checking overlap. Several current posts can support one commercial page, but two posts should not compete for the same exact query and intent.

### 4. Treatment pages need local, sober, conversion-oriented positioning

The live treatment pages are indexable and contain relevant content, but several titles are long or omit the clearest local treatment phrase. The live crawl flagged treatment titles between 61 and 69 characters. Recommended direction:

| Page | Primary cluster | Title direction |
|---|---|---|
| `/tratamentos/hemorroidas` | hemorroidas | `Tratamento de hemorroidas em Curitiba | Dra. Ana` |
| `/tratamentos/cx-laser` | laser | `Cirurgia a laser em proctologia em Curitiba` |
| `/tratamentos/cx-fistulas-anorretais` | fístula | `Tratamento de fístula anal em Curitiba` |
| `/tratamentos/toxina-botulinica` | fissura/toxina | `Toxina botulínica para fissura anal em Curitiba` |
| `/tratamentos/hpv-anal` | HPV/condiloma | `Tratamento de HPV anal em Curitiba` |
| `/tratamentos/cx-cisto-pilonidal` | cisto pilonidal | `Cirurgia de cisto pilonidal em Curitiba` |
| `/tratamentos/doencas-inflamatorias-intestinais` | DII | `Acompanhamento de DII em Curitiba` |
| `/tratamentos/sindrome-intestino-irritavel` | SII | `Tratamento de SII em Curitiba` |

These are directions, not automatically approved final medical copy. Titles, descriptions, H1s, and visible claims need a coordinated CFM/clinical review.

### 5. Some existing copy should receive a CFM-risk pass

Confirmed repository examples include phrases such as `tratamento definitivo`, `recuperação rápida`, `menor dor`, `melhor resultado`, and absolute statements in FAQs. These can overstate expected outcomes or obscure patient variability. Replace them with conditional, evidence-aligned language such as `em casos selecionados`, `pode`, `a indicação depende da avaliação`, and `resultados variam`, after clinical review.

Do not remove useful medical detail merely for SEO. The goal is accurate, sober communication, not weaker content.

## Technical and UX findings

### Confirmed or strongly supported

- All checked primary routes returned HTTP 200.
- `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` returned HTTP 200.
- The sitemap exposed 60 URLs; the crawl reached 61 pages.
- Core SEO, structured data, URL structure, local SEO, and content coverage are generally strong.
- Several treatment and article titles/descriptions exceed conventional snippet lengths.
- `/tratamentos` has about 210 words in the crawl and needs more decision-helpful content, not filler.
- Seven delivered images exceeded 200 KB; several PNG-derived blog assets were between roughly 315 KB and 646 KB after delivery.
- Potential LCP images lacked clear priority hints on key templates.
- The blog index exposes 151 internal links and all 44 cards at once; pagination or progressive loading should be evaluated for usability and crawl efficiency.
- Multiple templates were reported without a `<main>` landmark, and the mobile-menu dialog naming/ARIA associations need manual accessibility verification.

### Verify before changing

- The privacy page is intentionally `noindex`; its exclusion from the sitemap may be appropriate. Do not “fix” it only to satisfy a scanner.
- The crawler reported missing author and privacy signals, but visible pages contain author bylines and a privacy route. Inspect rendered HTML/schema before acting.
- Image dimension warnings may be false positives for Next.js `fill` images with reserved aspect-ratio containers. Verify layout shift in a real browser.
- A homepage canonical-chain warning appears to be a scanner normalization artifact; direct checks returned 200 at the trailing-slash URL.

### Days 31–60

1. Improve `/tratamentos` as a useful decision hub with clear distinctions between consultation, investigation, procedures, and follow-up.
2. Strengthen location journeys: consultation at Clínica Nassif and colonoscopy at Specta only when indicated.
3. Consolidate internal anchors so each treatment cluster has one primary owner.
4. Update existing thin or overlapping posts before commissioning new ones.
5. Validate Physician, MedicalOrganization, MedicalClinic, MedicalWebPage, Article, and Breadcrumb markup against visible facts. Do not add reviews or ratings without verified source data.

### Days 61–90

1. Use Search Console impressions and CTR to decide which priority-2 terms graduate to priority 1.
2. Replace weak zero-impression terms rather than continuously growing the tracker.
3. Add new content only for proven gaps with a distinct intent and target page.
4. Build legitimate local/entity citations and keep clinic details consistent with Google Business Profile and partner sites.

## Measurement plan

Use one consistent configuration from August onward:

- Device: mobile.
- Location: Curitiba.
- Search engine and language: unchanged across runs.
- Same 60-keyword portfolio for at least one full comparison period.

Track monthly:

- Priority-1 terms in Top 3 and Top 10.

- Treatment pages receiving impressions/clicks instead of the homepage.
- Organic consultation CTA events and qualified WhatsApp starts.
